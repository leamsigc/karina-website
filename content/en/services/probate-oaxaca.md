## When do you need probate in Oaxaca?

Probate is required when a person dies leaving assets that must pass formally to their heirs: a house, land, apartment, bank accounts, vehicles or possessory rights. Without a court judgment or a properly recorded will, heirs cannot register the property in their name, sell it or use it as collateral.

It is also needed when:

- There is no will and the heirs must be declared by a court.
- A will exists but the assets were never awarded.
- An heir objects or refuses to sign.
- Assets are located in several municipalities or on the coast.
- The property has no deed and possession must be regularized.

## Intestate or testate: which one applies

**Testate succession.** A valid will exists. The process confirms the named heirs, inventories the assets and awards them according to the testator's wishes. It is shorter and more predictable.

**Intestate succession.** No will. The court declares the heirs called by law —children, spouse or common-law partner, ascendants— and distributes the estate under intestate succession rules. It requires proof of kinship and sometimes an heir search.

If you are unsure which applies, the death certificate, the will (if any) and the property documents settle it at the first consultation.

## Stages of the proceeding

1. **Filing.** The file is submitted with the death certificate, kinship documents and a list of assets.
2. **Declaration of heirs.** The court recognizes the heirs and, where applicable, orders publications and notices.
3. **Inventory and appraisal.** Assets are listed and valued; experts and tax authorities may participate.
4. **Partition and award.** Each heir's share is defined and the award is ordered.
5. **Deed and registration.** The award is recorded before a notary and registered in the Public Property Registry.

Each stage can generate incidents. An heir who refuses to sign, a creditor's claim or an untitled property adds work but does not prevent the case from concluding.

## Documents to gather

- Death certificate.
- Will, if one exists.
- Birth and marriage certificates of the heirs.
- Official identification.
- Deeds, contracts, property tax bills or any document for the assets.
- List of bank accounts, vehicles and rights.
- Proof of *concubinato* if the heir is the partner.

If documents are missing, the court can request them or ask registries and authorities for information. Prior investigation avoids deficiencies and delays.

## How long it takes

An uncontested estate usually resolves in **6 to 12 months**. With opposing heirs, several properties or letters rogatory from another state, it takes longer. Notarial recording is a later step and usually takes a few additional weeks.

Timing depends less on the court than on the file: complete documents, timely appraisals and well-scheduled publications make the difference.

## Guideline cost

Fees depend on the number of assets, whether there is a will and whether there is a dispute. The ranges published in our probate guide are:

| Scenario | Fees | Fees, appraisal and notices | Guideline total |
|---|---|---|---|
| Simple intestate (1 house, 2-3 agreeing heirs) | $18,000 – $28,000 | $3,000 – $6,000 | **$21,000 – $34,000** |

## Special situations we handle

**Property without a deed.** Probate can cover possessory rights; usucapión is then filed to register title. [See the usucapión guide](/en/blog/adverse-possession-usucapion-oaxaca-requirements).

**Opposing heir or refusal to sign.** The case continues; the objection is resolved with evidence within the proceeding.

**Heirs abroad.** Kinship is proven with apostilled documents and powers of attorney are arranged for representation.

**Coastal property.** Fideicomisos, federal zone restrictions and the property regime are reviewed before the award.

**Debts of the deceased.** Inventory and award must consider liens and creditors to avoid surprises for the heir.

## Frequently asked questions

### How long does probate take in Oaxaca?
An uncontested estate usually takes 6 to 12 months. Opposing heirs, several properties or letters rogatory from another state can extend it. Notarial deed recording is a later step after the award.

### How much does probate cost in Oaxaca?
It depends on whether the estate is intestate or testate, on the number of properties and on whether there is a dispute. As a guideline, a simple intestate estate is around 21,000 to 34,000 pesos plus fees and appraisals; a contested one can exceed 50,000. A written budget is provided.

### What happens if there is no will?
An intestate succession is filed. The court declares the heirs called by law —children, spouse or common-law partner, ascendants— and distributes the estate under the rules of intestate succession.

### Can probate proceed if one heir refuses to sign?
Yes. Probate is a court proceeding and continues even if an heir refuses to sign or objects. The objection is resolved within the same proceeding and does not block the award indefinitely.

### Is a foreign will valid in Mexico?
It can be, but it requires judicial recognition (exequátur) and certified translations. For cost and time it is usually better to execute a public open will in Mexico before a notary, recorded in the National Registry of Wills.
