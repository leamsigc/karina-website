## What determines the cost of a divorce in Oaxaca?

There is no single price — every case is quoted on complexity. These are the factors that actually move the cost:

- **Mutual consent vs contested.** A [mutual-consent divorce](/en/divorce-lawyer-oaxaca) with a signed agreement needs fewer hearings and resolves in **1 to 3 months**. A unilateral divorce without agreements means a full lawsuit with evidence and can take **6 to 18 months**.
- **Minor children.** [Child support](/en/services/child-support-alimony-lawyer-oaxaca), custody and visitation add stages (and cost) to the process.
- **Shared assets.** Liquidating the marital partnership may require inventory and property appraisals.
- **Where the parties live.** If a spouse lives outside Oaxaca or abroad, interstate or international court communications are needed — possible without traveling through a power of attorney.

## What a serious divorce quote includes

Beware of anyone quoting a price over the phone without knowing your case. A professional quote must include, in writing: fees per stage, estimated court and Civil Registry costs, likely timelines and payment options. That is exactly what you receive in your **free first consultation** with us.
