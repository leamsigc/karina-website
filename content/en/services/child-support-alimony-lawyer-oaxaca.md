# Child Support & Alimony Attorney in Oaxaca

Child support (*pensión alimenticia*) in the State of Oaxaca is a strictly enforced legal right designed to secure living expenses, education, health care, and housing for children and eligible spouses.

Whether you are a local resident or an expat living in Oaxaca, our firm provides aggressive legal representation in Family Courts (*Juzgados de lo Familiar*) across Oaxaca City, Puerto Escondido, Huatulco, and surrounding municipalities.

## Core Child Support Services

### 1. Emergency Temporary Child Support Orders
We file expedited motions for immediate temporary support (*pensión provisional*) at the time the petition is submitted, ensuring funds are secured without waiting months for a final verdict.

### 2. Direct Wage Garnishment Orders
Our legal team secures judicial payroll withholding orders served directly to the obligor's employer, collecting monthly support payments, annual bonuses, and benefits automatically.

### 3. Modifications & Inflation Adjustments
Filing for legitimate support increases based on inflation metrics, rising educational requirements (college or private school tuition), or medical expenses.

### 4. Enforcement Against Non-Compliant Obligors
For unpaid child support arrears, we initiate asset restraint measures (liens on real estate, bank account freezes) and criminal enforcement under the Civil & Penal Codes of Oaxaca.

## How Child Support is Calculated in Oaxaca

Oaxaca law strictly enforces the **Principle of Proportionality**: support must match the recipient's financial needs and the paying parent's income capacity.

| Parent's Employment Status | Judicial Enforcement Method | Typical Percentage per Child |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Salaried Employee** | Direct payroll deduction via court mandate | **15% to 25%** per dependent |
| **Independent / Business Owner** | Standard of living asset audit & bank trace | Fixed monthly UMA / Minimum Wage multiples |
| **Multiple Dependents** | Proportional equitable cap | Max **50% to 60%** of net earnings |

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Can child support be enforced against foreign currency or international income?
Yes. Courts in Oaxaca can order support based on verified foreign income, bank accounts, or local business revenues owned by non-Mexican citizens residing in Mexico.

### What happens if the paying parent moves away from Oaxaca?
Mexican Family Law provides inter-state and international enforcement mechanisms, allowing child support judgments to be executed across all 32 Mexican states.
