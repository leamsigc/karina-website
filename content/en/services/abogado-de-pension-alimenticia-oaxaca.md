# Specialized Child Support Lawyer in Oaxaca

Child support (*pensión alimenticia*) is a non-waivable priority legal right designed to guarantee food, housing, clothing, medical care, and education for children and financial dependents in the State of Oaxaca.

We provide comprehensive legal services to enforce compliance, petition for timely support increases, seek legally justified reductions, or defend against excessive wage garnishment in the Family Courts of the Central Judicial District and across Oaxaca.

## Child Support Legal Services

### 1. Provisional and Permanent Child Support Lawsuits
Immediate court actions before the Family Judge to secure **provisional child support from the initial filing of the lawsuit**, ensuring fast financial relief for minor children.

### 2. Direct Wage Garnishment & Benefits Withholding
Court orders issued directly to the obligor's employer for direct payroll deductions (percentage of base salary plus ordinary and extraordinary bonuses).

### 3. Support Increase and Inflation Adjustment
Petitions for statutory cost-of-living increases (matching the Consumer Price Index) or documented increases in the dependent's needs (university tuition, specialized medical expenses).

### 4. Recovery of Past-Due or Retroactive Child Support
Legal actions to collect unpaid arrears and attach assets (real estate, bank accounts, or vehicles) to settle unpaid child support debts.

## How Child Support is Calculated in Oaxaca

In the State of Oaxaca, there is no single fixed percentage. Instead, Family Court judges apply the **proportionality rule**: *support must be awarded in proportion to the needs of the recipient and the financial capacity of the obligor*.

| Employment Status | Application Criteria | Average Percentage per Child |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Salaried Employee** | Direct payroll deduction on net income | **15% to 25%** per dependent |
| **Self-Employed / Professional** | Financial lifestyle audit, bank checks, and Public Registry search | Fixed multiple of minimum wage or UMA units |
| **Multiple Children / Dependents** | Proportional ceiling protecting the obligor's vital minimum | Maximum **50% to 60%** of total net income |

## Frequently Asked Questions About Child Support in Oaxaca

### What happens if the obligor quits their job to avoid paying support?
Intentional resignation or simulated insolvency to evade child support constitutes a criminal offense against family obligations under the Oaxaca Penal Code, punishable by imprisonment and asset seizure.

### Up to what age must child support be paid?
Until the child reaches adulthood (18 years old), or until completion of technical/university studies within a reasonable timeframe and maintaining passing grades.
