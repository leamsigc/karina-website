## Frequently Asked Questions About Divorce in Oaxaca

### What if my spouse refuses to sign or does not want a divorce?
In Oaxaca, **the divorce will be granted regardless**. Your spouse's refusal cannot stop the dissolution of the marriage. Court proceedings will simply continue regarding unresolved points (such as custody or assets), but the divorce decree itself is inevitable.

### How much does a divorce cost in Oaxaca?
Costs depend on whether there is mutual agreement on custody and support or if contested litigation is required. During our initial consultation, we evaluate your case and provide a fixed quote with flexible payment options.

### Can I file for divorce if I live in another state or abroad?
Yes. We can represent you via a Power of Attorney (Poder Notarial) to handle the entire legal proceeding in the Oaxaca courts without requiring you to travel constantly.
