## Probate, inheritance and property

### How long does probate take in Oaxaca?

An uncontested probate usually takes 6 to 12 months; with opposing heirs, several properties or letters rogatory from another state it can take longer. The stages are admission, inventory, partition and award; notarial deed recording is a later step.

[Probate guide →](/en/blog/probate-succession-oaxaca-intestate-testate-cost)

### What happens if there is no will?

An intestate succession is filed before the Civil Court: legal heirs — children, spouse or common-law partner, ascendants — are declared and receive the estate under the law. Without a will the process is longer and requires proof of kinship.

### Is a foreign will valid in Mexico?

It can be, but enforcing it requires a recognition proceeding (exequátur) and certified translations. For cost and time, it is usually better to execute a public open will in Mexico before a notary, recorded in the National Registry of Wills (RNAT).

[Wills for expats →](/en/blog/expat-estate-planning-wills-oaxaca)

### What is usucapión and how long does it take?

It is the lawsuit that converts long possession into registered ownership. Five years of good-faith possession or ten years of bad-faith possession are considered, with requirements of publicity, peace and continuity. The case usually takes 12 to 24 months depending on the court and the evidence.

[Usucapión guide →](/en/blog/adverse-possession-usucapion-oaxaca-requirements)

### Can I regularize land without a deed?

Yes, through usucapión if possession is proven, or through municipal and notarial procedures when a prior title exists. The route depends on the property’s history, the land regime (private, ejidal or communal) and the documents available.

[Regularization case →](/en/case-studies/regularizacion-propiedad-costera)
