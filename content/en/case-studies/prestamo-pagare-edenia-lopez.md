::CaseStudyDetailContent
---
challenge: "Ms. Edenia López faced a critical financial situation: she had lent a significant sum of money backed by a signed promissory note (*pagaré*). However, upon maturity, the debtors systematically refused to honor their payment obligation, putting our client's financial stability at risk."

strategy: "We implemented an assertive commercial lawsuit strategy (*Juicio Ejecutivo Mercantil*). Leveraging the promissory note's legal status as an executive title carrying immediate execution, we requested a precautionary asset attachment (*embargo precautorio*) at the outset of proceedings to guarantee recovery. We maintained close procedural monitoring in the Oaxaca de Juárez courts to eliminate delays."

outcome: "Thanks to flawless execution, we obtained a court ruling ordering the immediate repayment of the principal loan amount, as well as default interest (*intereses moratorios*) accrued during the entire period of non-payment. This case highlights the importance of timely legal counsel in enforcing negotiable instruments to protect family assets."
