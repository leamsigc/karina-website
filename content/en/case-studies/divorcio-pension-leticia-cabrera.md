---
challenge: "Ms. Leticia Cabrera needed to formalize her marital status through divorce, but the primary challenge was guaranteeing the future financial well-being of her minor children. A prolonged or mismanaged judicial process could have resulted in unnecessary family distress and uncertainty regarding child support."

strategy: "We implemented a strategy focused on pre-litigation resolution and conflict prevention. We drafted a comprehensive divorce settlement agreement detailing the rights and responsibilities of both parties. This agreement not only resolved the marital dissolution, but also established a child support amount proportional to the children's needs and the parents' financial capacity, along with a healthy visitation schedule."

outcome: "The result was a swift and effective decree before the Family Courts of Oaxaca de Juárez. A multi-year high-conflict lawsuit was completely avoided, obtaining a favorable court decree in record time. This case demonstrates the value of legal representation that protects the client's rights while prioritizing the best interest of minor children."
