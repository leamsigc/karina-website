## Probate in Oaxaca: inherit without breaking the family

Without **probate in Oaxaca**, house and accounts don't pass to children — even if "everyone knows it was Dad's". Without will: 10-year claim window but fights start in 10 days.

**In 45 words for Google**: **Intestate** (no will) identifies heirs, appoints executor, inventories, awards. **6-12 months uncontested, 12-18 contested**. Costs **$18k-$45k**. Testate faster (4-8 months). Filed at Civil-Family Courts.

## Intestate vs Testate

|  | **Intestate** (70% in Oaxaca) | **Testate** |
|---|---|---|
| When | No will | Notarized will |
| Judge does | Determines legal heirs: children+spouse, then parents | Validates will, respects wishes |
| Time | 6-12 months | 4-8 months |
| Cost | $18k-$35k | $15k-$28k |

---

## Requirements

**Intestate:** death certificate, birth/marriage certs of heirs, property deeds/tax, ID, negative will certificate (we get).

**Testate:** plus original will.

Photos via WhatsApp enough for diagnosis.

---

## Stages (4 sections)

1. **Heir declaration (8-12 weeks)** — petition + edicts 3×, judge declares heirs + executor
2. **Inventory & appraisal (4-6 weeks)** — executor lists + values
3. **Administration** — executor manages, pays tax, accounts
4. **Partition & award (4-8 weeks)** — judge awards + notary deed

No unanimity needed — notified heirs in default, case continues.
