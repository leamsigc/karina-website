## What is No-Fault Divorce (Express Divorce) in Oaxaca?

A **no-fault divorce in Oaxaca** (*Divorcio Incausado* or express divorce) allows you to dissolve your marriage **without assigning fault or providing grounds** before a judge. You no longer need to prove allegations such as adultery, abandonment, or cruelty. **Your desire to end the marriage is legally sufficient.**

Under current Oaxaca family law reforms, **the judge is obligated to grant the divorce decree** upon your ratification, without forcing reconciliation hearings.

---

## 📋 Requirements for No-Fault Divorce in Oaxaca (2026)

Gathering your documents is the first step toward a fast resolution:

- **Marriage Certificate:** Recent certified copy issued by the Civil Registry.
- **Official Identification (Passport or Voter ID):** Of the petitioning spouse.
- **Birth Certificates of Minor Children:** Required if children are under 18.
- **Proposed Settlement Agreement (*Propuesta de Convenio*):** A mandatory legal document establishing:
  - **Child Custody:** Where the minors will reside.
  - **Child Support:** Amount, percentage, and payment schedule.
  - **Visitation Schedule:** Approved days and holiday schedules.
  - **Marital Residence:** Which spouse remains in the home.
  - **Property Division:** Liquidation of community marital property (if applicable).
- **Proof of Income & Address:** Useful if filing for provisional child support.

**⚠️ Key Fact:** You do NOT need witnesses, photos, or **your spouse's signature**. Your consent alone is legally sufficient!

---

## ⏱️ How Long Does an Express Divorce Take in Oaxaca?

Timelines depend primarily on whether mutual agreement exists between the parties. Based on trial practice in the **Oaxaca Central Family Courts**, current 2026 timelines are:

- **Mutual Agreement Divorce (children & property resolved):** **1 to 3 months**.
- **Unilateral Divorce without children or property:** **6 to 10 weeks**.
- **Contested Unilateral Divorce (litigation over custody or support):** **4 to 12 months**.
- **Unlocatable Spouse (service of process via publication):** Adds **2 to 3 months**.

## 💡 3 Critical Mistakes to Avoid in Your Divorce

To avoid procedural delays in Ciudad Judicial courts, avoid the following:

1. **Using generic templates from the internet:** Submitting flawed agreements that fail to safeguard children's best interests leads to immediate judicial rejection.
2. **Improper Service Address:** Providing an incorrect address for your spouse stalls proceedings. We perform preliminary institutional location searches.
3. **Failing to Request Provisional Support:** A skilled trial lawyer files urgent motions (like provisional child support) from Day 1 to secure your income.
