For foreign citizens and expats living in the US, Canada, or Europe who married in Oaxaca (or whose spouse currently resides in the state), **divorce proceedings can be executed 100% remotely without traveling to Mexico**.

Through the **Consular Power of Attorney** procedure, our law firm assumes complete legal representation before the Family Courts in Oaxaca.

---

## ✈️ How to Obtain a Divorce in Oaxaca Without Traveling to Mexico

The international legal process involves the following key steps:

1. **Virtual Legal Consultation:** We analyze your marital circumstances, minor children, and real estate/financial assets acquired during marriage.
2. **Drafting the Power of Attorney Draft:** We draft the exact legal text for the *General Power of Attorney for Lawsuits and Collections with Special Clause for No-Fault Divorce*.
3. **Mexican Consulate Appointment:** You sign and protocolize the document at the nearest Mexican Consulate in your home country.
4. **Filing & Court Proceedings:** You mail the original document to our law office in Oaxaca, and we file the formal divorce lawsuit in Family Court.

---

## ⚖️ Requirements for Expat Divorce in Oaxaca

- **Marriage Certificate:** Certified copy from the Oaxaca Civil Registry or foreign marriage certificate **apostilled and translated by an official certified court translator**.
- **Official Identification:** Valid passport or official photo ID.
- **Consular Power of Attorney:** Executed before the Mexican Consular Network.
- **Divorce Settlement Agreement:** Proposed custody, support, visitation, and property division terms.

---

## 🌐 Enforceability of the Final Decree Abroad

Once the final divorce decree is issued by the Oaxaca Family Court Judge:
- An official marginal note is registered on the marriage certificate at the Civil Registry.
- We issue certified copies of the court judgment.
- We obtain the **Oaxaca State Apostille** on the judgment for international recognition (*Exequatur*) in your home country.
