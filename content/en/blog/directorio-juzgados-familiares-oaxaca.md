# Family Courts in Oaxaca: Directory & Legal Procedures 2026

Filing or responding to **divorce proceedings, child custody disputes, alimony claims, or probate cases** in Oaxaca requires navigating the **Family Courts** (*Juzgados de lo Familiar*) under the High Court of Justice of the State of Oaxaca (TSJO).

---

## 1. Central Judicial District Family Courts

For individuals residing in Oaxaca City, Xoxocotlán, Santa Lucía, and surrounding Central Valleys, jurisdiction belongs to the Central District Family Courts:

- **Main Judicial Complex**: Reyes Mantecón Judicial Center, San Bartolo Coyotepec, Oaxaca.
- **Subject Matter Jurisdiction**: No-fault divorces (*divorcio incausado*), child support enforcement, custody orders, visitation rights, and estate inheritance.

---

## 2. Common Judicial Proceedings Handled

- **Uncontested & Contested Divorces**: Submission of settlement proposals and asset division.
- **Immediate Child Support Orders**: Issuance of payroll garnishment orders to employers.
- **Emergency Custody Injunctions**: Temporary protective orders for minors and domestic stability.
- **Testate & Intestate Probate Trials**: Formal liquidation and distribution of real estate and financial assets.

---

## 3. How Case Tracking Works in Oaxaca

1. **Official Judicial Bulletin**: Daily publication of court rulings and official notices by judges.
2. **Physical Docket Review**: Inspection of physical court files carried out by authorized counsel.
3. **Legal Representation**: Engaging a licensed local attorney ensures prompt filing and active monitoring.
