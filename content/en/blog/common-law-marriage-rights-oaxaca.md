Under the **Civil Code of the State of Oaxaca** and recent rulings by the Supreme Court of Mexico (SCJN), **common-law marriage (*concubinato*)** grants partners statutory family and property protections **virtually identical to formal marriage**.

Whether you are an expat cohabitating with a Mexican citizen or a long-term resident in Oaxaca, understanding your legal rights regarding property, inheritance, palimony, and official registration is essential.

---

## 📜 Requisites for Legal Concubinato Recognition in Oaxaca

To establish a legally enforceable concubinato in Oaxaca, the relationship must satisfy specific statutory criteria:

1. **Continuous Cohabitation:** Uninterrupted living together as a couple for a minimum of **2 consecutive years**.
2. **Having Children Together:** If the couple has children in common, **no minimum timeframe is required**—concubinato status exists from birth.
3. **No Legal Impediments to Marriage:** Both partners must be legally single (unmarried or legally divorced).

---

## 💡 Enforceable Legal Rights for Common-Law Partners

Once concubinato status is proven before an Oaxaca court or notary, partners unlock key rights:

### 1. Compensatory Spousal Support (Palimony)
Upon separation, a partner who devoted themselves primarily to domestic duties or child-rearing is entitled to **monthly support payments for a duration equivalent to the relationship's length**.

### 2. Intestate Inheritance Rights
If a partner passes away without leaving a will in Mexico, the surviving concubino/a has **first-tier statutory inheritance rights equivalent to a legal spouse**, taking precedence over distant relatives.

### 3. Social Security & Pension Benefits
Right to enrollment as a dependent under public health systems (IMSS, ISSSTE) and entitlement to survivor pensions or work-injury indemnities.

### 4. Equitable Asset Compensation
If assets were acquired during the partnership while one partner managed the home, Family Court judges in Oaxaca can award **financial compensation up to 50% of acquired assets**.

---

## 📝 Obtaining an Official Concubinato Certificate in Oaxaca

To demonstrate common-law status before immigration authorities, banks, or insurance providers:

- **Judicial Voluntaria Proceeding:** Formal court order issued by an Oaxaca Family Judge based on documentary evidence and witness testimony.
- **Notarial Instrument (*Escritura Notarial*):** Executed before an official Mexican Notary Public (*Notario Público*).
