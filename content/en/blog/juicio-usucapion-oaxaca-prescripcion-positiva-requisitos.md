## Adverse Possession in Oaxaca: Converting Possession into Registered Title Deeds

Thousands of property owners across **Xoxocotlán, Guadalupe Victoria, El Rosario, Pueblo Nuevo, San Jacinto Amilpas, and the coast of Huatulco/Puerto Escondido** have lived for decades on land **without registered public title deeds** — holding only private purchase contracts or informal handwritten documents. **Adverse Possession (*Usucapión* or *Prescripción Positiva*)** is the civil court lawsuit that converts continuous physical possession into an **officially registered title deed in the Public Property Registry**.

Statutory requirements mandate **5 years of open, peaceful, continuous possession as owner under good faith (with private contract) or 10 years (without written title)**. Lawsuits take **12–24 months**, cost **$32k–$56k MXN**, and conclude with registered public deeds. *Note: Does not apply to ejidal land.*

---

## ⚖️ Statutory Requirements under the Oaxaca Civil Code

Under Article 1168 of the Oaxaca Civil Code, **Positive Prescription** rewards long-term property possession:

1. **Public Possession:** Open to the public view, not hidden or clandestine.
2. **Peaceful Possession:** Acquired and maintained without force or unlawful eviction.
3. **Continuous Possession:** Uninterrupted occupancy without abandonment.
4. **Owner Capacity (*En Concepto de Dueño*):** Acting publicly as the owner (paying property taxes, maintaining boundaries).
5. **Statutory Time Limit:** **5 years** with just title (private contract, gift) under good faith, or **10 years** without written title under bad faith.

---

## 📋 Required Documents & Evidence Checklist

Items required for initial filing:

- **Private Contract / Proof of Purchase:** Serves as legal just title (*justo título*).
- **Property Tax Receipts (*Predial*):** Receipts for the past **5+ years** in your name or the predecessor's name.
- **Utility Receipts (CFE Electricity / Water):** Proving uninterrupted residential service.
- **Official ID & Proof of Address:** Matching the property address.
- **Topographical Survey Plan:** Prepared by a licensed surveyor with exact boundary measurements.
- **Witnesses:** 2–3 neighboring property owners testifying to your continuous ownership for 5–10 years.

---

## ⏱️ Real Court Procedure in Oaxaca Civil Courts

1. **Diagnostic Review:** Confirming 5 vs. 10-year rules, private vs. ejidal status, and evidence gaps.
2. **Filing the Lawsuit:** Submitting the petition before the Central Civil Courts of Oaxaca in Ciudad Judicial.
3. **Publication of Court Notices (*Edictos*):** Statutory newspaper publications allowing unknown interested parties to appear.
4. **Default / Answer Period:** Service of process on the registered record owner.
5. **Evidentiary Stage:** Witness testimony, surveyor appraisals, and document review.
6. **Final Court Decree:** The judge issues the judgment declaring full legal ownership.
7. **Registry Recording:** Final judgment recording at the **Public Property and Commerce Registry of Oaxaca (IFREO)**, serving as your official registered title deed.
