# Child Support & Alimony Calculation in Oaxaca, Mexico: 2026 Legal Guide

**Child support (*Pensión Alimenticia*) in Oaxaca** is a mandatory constitutional duty designed to ensure children and eligible dependents receive comprehensive support covering education, housing, healthcare, clothing, and sustenance.

---

## 💡 How Child Support Amounts Are Calculated in Oaxaca

Mexican law does not enforce an arbitrary fixed rate; instead, **Family Courts in Oaxaca** apply the **Principle of Proportionality**, balancing the child's financial needs against the obligor's actual income and capacity.

### Standard Judicial Benchmarks:
- **1 Child:** **15% to 25%** of net income.
- **2 Children:** **25% to 35%** of net income.
- **3+ Children:** **40% to 50%** of net income (capping at reasonable thresholds to preserve basic living standards).

---

## 💼 Self-Employed or Informal Income Obligors

If the paying parent is self-employed, an independent contractor, or conceals formal payroll receipts:
- The court assigns a mandatory monthly support quota calculated against the official minimum wage and baseline cost of living.
- Judicial audits inspect bank records, property ownership, tax filings, lifestyle indicators, and credit card usage to establish true financial capacity.

---

## 📈 Modifying Child Support Orders (Increase or Reduction)

Support decrees can be formally modified whenever financial or personal circumstances undergo substantial shifts:

### Grounds for Increasing Support:
- Higher educational expenses (e.g., entering high school or university).
- Ongoing specialized medical treatments.
- Proven salary increases or new business ventures of the paying parent.

### Grounds for Requesting Reductions:
- Birth of additional children legally entitled to support.
- Severe disability or involuntary loss of employment.

---

## ⚖️ Enforcement & Penalties for Non-Payment

- **Direct Wage Garnishment** from payroll, bonuses, and severance pay.
- **Asset Seizure** of bank accounts, vehicles, and real estate properties.
- **Inclusion in the Registry of Delinquent Alimony Obligors.**
- **Criminal Penalties:** Failure to fulfill family support duties carries **1 to 4 years imprisonment** under the Oaxaca State Penal Code.
