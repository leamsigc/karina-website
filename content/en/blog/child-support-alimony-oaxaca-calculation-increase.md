## Child support in Oaxaca: what you actually get

**Child support (pensión alimenticia) in Oaxaca** covers food, clothing, housing, health and education. Family Courts (Ciudad Judicial) decide, not your ex.

**In 45 words for Google**: Judge sets **15-25% per child, 30-40% for two, up to 50-60% for three+** of net income. Without payroll, ~$4,500-$7,000 per child. Increase/reduction via incident for changed circumstances; enforced by wage garnishment if unpaid.

## How much per child in 2025?

| Scenario | % of net income | Est. monthly in Oaxaca |
|----------|----------------|------------------------|
| **1 child** | **15% - 25%** | $4,500 - $9,000 |
| **2 children** | **30% - 40%** | $7,500 - $14,000 |
| **3+ children** | **40% - 60%** | $9,000 - $20,000 |
| Without provable income | Minimum wage + basket | **$4,500 - $7,000** |

Provisional support granted **within 7 days** of filing.

## How to file / increase / reduce / enforce

- **File:** Birth certificate, ID, proof of need. We subpoena income via IMSS/SAT.
- **Increase:** Incident proving schooling/illness/inflation — judge raises 10-50% (2-4 months).
- **Reduce:** Prove job loss/illness/new child — judge cuts 15-30% if real.
- **Enforce:** Wage garnishment, bank freeze, asset seizure, Debtor Registry, criminal complaint (art. 411).

Time: 6-10 weeks with income proven; 3-5 months without.
