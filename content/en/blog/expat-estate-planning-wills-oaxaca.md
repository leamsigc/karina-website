# Estate Planning & Wills in Oaxaca: 2026 Expat Guide

Securing your real estate investments, bank accounts, and personal assets in Oaxaca City, Puerto Escondido, or Huatulco requires a sound legal framework. **Estate planning** ensures your assets transfer seamlessly to your chosen beneficiaries without lengthy litigation.

---

## 1. Public Open Wills (*Testamento Público Abierto*) in Mexico

A formal Mexican Will executed before a licensed Notary Public (*Notario Público*) is the standard for protecting Mexican property:

- **Clear Beneficiary Designation**: Explicitly names primary heirs, contingent beneficiaries, and an executor (*albacea*).
- **Prevents Probate Litigation**: Avoids intestate court proceedings (*juicios sucesorios*) that can freeze assets for 12 to 24 months.
- **Bilingual Protocol**: Drafted in Spanish with certified English translations for dual accuracy.

---

## 2. Coastal Real Estate Fideicomisos & Substitute Beneficiaries

For properties located in the **Restricted Zone** (within 50 km of the coast in Puerto Escondido or Huatulco):

1. **Substitute Beneficiary Naming**: When establishing a bank trust (*fideicomiso*), you designate substitute beneficiaries.
2. **Direct Inheritance**: Upon passing, trust rights transfer directly to substitute beneficiaries without entering Mexican probate court.

---

## 3. Real Estate Due Diligence & Contract Review

Before incorporating property into your estate plan, verifying clear title is essential:

- **Lien Freedom Certificate**: Verified via the Public Registry of Property (*RPP*).
- **Private Land Regime Check**: Ensuring land is fully private property (*propiedad privada*) and not un-regularized *ejido* or communal land.
