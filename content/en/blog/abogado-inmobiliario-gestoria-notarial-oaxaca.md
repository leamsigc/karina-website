Acquiring a home, land parcel, or commercial property in **Oaxaca City, Puerto Escondido, or Huatulco** represents a valuable financial investment. However, the real estate market in Oaxaca involves unique legal complexities, such as the coexistence of **Private Property (*Propiedad Privada*), Ejidal Land (*Propiedad Ejidal*), and Communal Land (*Propiedad Comunal*)**.

Working with a specialized **Real Estate Attorney and independent Notary Closing Manager** is the only way to avoid title fraud, hidden liens, and future litigation.

---

## 🔍 Real Estate Legal Due Diligence

Before signing purchase contracts or transferring deposits, we execute a rigorous title audit:

1. **Title Search at the Property Registry (IFREO):** Official search at the Public Property Registry of Oaxaca to verify the seller's title deeds and ensure there are no liens, mortgages, or court attachments.
2. **Land-Use & Municipal Permit Audit:** Verification of zoning regulations with Municipal Urban Development offices.
3. **Agrarian Land Status Verification:** Legal confirmation that the property is NOT ejidal or communal land lacking proper National Agrarian Registry (*RAN*) privatization certificates.

---

## 📜 Notary Closing & Public Deed Services in Oaxaca

We guide buyers and sellers through every stage of the notary closing process:

- **Purchase & Sale Contract Drafting:** Inclusion of protective clauses, earnest money terms, and performance guarantees.
- **Obtaining Closing Certificates:** Certificate of No Liens (*Libertad de Gravamen*), Tax Appraisals, and Municipal Property Tax/Water No-Debt clearances.
- **Notary Closing Protocolization:** Coordinating execution of the Public Deed (*Escritura Pública*) before a licensed Notary Public and calculating property transfer taxes (ISAI & ISR).

---

## 🏖️ Bank Trusts (*Fideicomisos*) for Foreigners on the Oaxaca Coast

Under Article 27 of the Mexican Constitution, properties within **50 kilometers of the coastline (Puerto Escondido, Mazunte, Zipolite, Huatulco)** reside in the Restricted Zone:

- We obtain acquisition permits from the **Ministry of Foreign Affairs (SRE)**.
- We establish the **Real Estate Bank Trust (*Fideicomiso Inmobiliario*)**, securing 100% legal ownership rights for foreign investors for renewable 50-year periods.
