**Concubinato (common-law marriage) in Oaxaca** is fully recognized and protected under the State Civil Code and Supreme Court of Justice rulings, granting **family and property rights virtually identical to formal marriage**.

If you have lived in a common-law relationship in Oaxaca and wish to formalize your legal status, obtain an official concubinato certificate, claim alimony, or enforce inheritance rights, this legal guide explains your full legal standing.

---

## 📜 Legal Requirements for Concubinato Recognition in Oaxaca

For a common-law relationship to produce full legal effects in Oaxaca, the following legal criteria must be met:

1. **Uninterrupted Cohabitation:** Living together as a couple for a minimum of **2 continuous years**.
2. **Procreation of Children:** If the couple has children in common, **no minimum time limit applies**; concubinato status exists from the child's birth.
3. **Absence of Legal Impediments:** Both partners must be legally single (unmarried or divorced).

---

## 💡 Legally Guaranteed Rights for Common-Law Partners

Once concubinato status is established in Oaxaca, Mexican law guarantees the following core rights:

### 1. Compensatory Alimony Support
Upon separation, the partner who dedicated themselves primarily to homemaking or child-rearing is entitled to **monthly compensatory alimony for a duration equal to the length of the cohabitation**.

### 2. Intestate Inheritance Rights
In the event of a partner's death without a will, the surviving common-law spouse has **equal inheritance rights as a legal spouse**, holding priority over distant relatives.

### 3. Social Security & Pension Benefits
Right to registration as a beneficiary for healthcare (IMSS, ISSSTE, PENSIONES), as well as entitlement to survivor pensions and workplace accident compensation.

### 4. Equitable Asset Compensation
If assets were acquired during the relationship and one partner managed household duties, Oaxaca Family Court judges authorize **financial compensation of up to 50% of assets acquired**.

---

## 📝 How to Obtain a Concubinato Certificate in Oaxaca

To formally prove common-law marriage before public agencies or banks:

- **Notarial Declaration / Family Court Motion:** Judicial declaration or notary deed executed with neighbor witness testimony.
- **Civil Registry Registration:** Formal registration of concubinato status to obtain official certificates.
