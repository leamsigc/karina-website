## No-fault divorce — 1-3 months with agreement

- **No grounds, no blame.** One spouse's will is enough.
- **Mutual:** both sign + agreement → **4-7 weeks**
- **Unilateral without kids/assets:** **6-10 weeks**
- **Contested custody/support:** **5-12 months** (but divorce itself decreed after 3-day ratification)

**Requirements:** marriage certificate, ID, kids' birth certs, proposed agreement (custody/support/visits/property). Power of attorney via apostille if you're abroad — **no travel needed**.

## Child custody — father can win

**Myth:** "Judge always gives kids to mother." **False since 2017** (SCJN). Father wins **32%** proving stability, availability, housing, support network and psychological fitness.

- **Sole custody** (one parent) — most common
- **Joint/shared** — week/week or alt weekends, both fit, live <30 min from school, can coordinate — **22% in 2024** and rising

Typical visitation if non-custodial: **alt Fri 18:00–Sun 18:00 + one weekday + split holidays**.
