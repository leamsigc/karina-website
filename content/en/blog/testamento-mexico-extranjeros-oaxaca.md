If you own real estate, bank accounts, corporate shares, or bank trusts (*Fideicomisos*) in **Oaxaca City, Puerto Escondido, or Huatulco**, the most prudent estate planning step is executing an **Open Public Will (*Testamento Público Abierto*) before a Notary Public in Mexico**.

Prevent your loved ones from facing complex and costly **Intestate Probate Proceedings (*Juicio Sucesorio Intestamentario*)** that can drag on for years in Oaxaca courts.

---

## 🏛️ Why a Foreign Will is Not Enough in Mexico

While a will executed in the US, Canada, or Europe is legally recognized in theory:

- Executing a foreign will in Oaxaca requires an expensive court validation proceeding (*Exequátur*).
- It mandates **international Apostilles and certified court translations in Mexico**.
- A **Mexican Will executed before a local Notary** is recorded in the **National Will Notice Registry (*RNAT*)** and local property registries, enabling direct estate transfer without court litigation.

---

## 📜 Key Provisions in a Mexican Will

An Open Public Will allows you to clearly specify the distribution of your assets:

1. **Universal Heirs (*Herederos Universales*):** Beneficiaries who receive total percentage shares of the estate.
2. **Specific Legatees (*Legatarios*):** Beneficiaries designated to receive specific assets (e.g., a condo in Oaxaca or a specific bank account).
3. **Estate Executor (*Albacea*):** The trusted individual or law firm appointed to administer the estate and transfer property title to heirs.

---

## 📝 Requirements to Execute a Will in Oaxaca

- **Valid Official ID:** Passport (for foreigners) or Mexican Residency Card.
- **List of Assets & Property Title Details:** Deeds, contracts, or bank trust details.
- **List of Beneficiaries & Alternate Heirs:** Full legal names and relationship details.
- **Certified Translator:** Required if the testator does not speak or read Spanish.
