# Child Support in Oaxaca: Legal Guide, Calculations & Percentages (2026)

**Child support (*pensión alimenticia*) in Oaxaca** is a strictly enforced legal duty guaranteed under the Mexican Constitution and the Oaxaca Civil Code. Its primary objective is to secure the overall living standards and welfare of children and eligible dependents.

---

## 💡 How Child Support is Calculated in Oaxaca

Oaxaca law does not impose a single rigid percentage. Instead, **Family Courts in Oaxaca** apply the **Principle of Proportionality**: evaluating the financial needs of the recipient against the earning capacity of the obligor.

### Frequent Benchmark Percentages:
- **1 Child:** Between **15% and 25%** of verifiable net income.
- **2 Children:** Between **25% and 35%** of net salary.
- **3 or More Children:** From **40% up to 50%** (a statutory guideline cap protecting the obligor's basic sustenance).

---

## 💼 Obligors Without Verifiable Income (Self-Employed or Informal Sector)

If the defendant is self-employed or claims to lack a fixed payroll:
- The judge does NOT excuse payment obligations.
- Support is awarded based on **UMA (Economic Measurement Unit)** multiples, the current minimum wage, and court social work lifestyle audits.
- Standard of living is inferred through bank statements, vehicle ownership, credit card usage, and financial records.

---

## 📈 Requesting a Support Increase or Reduction

Financial circumstances change over time and can be modified via incidental court proceedings:

### Grounds for Requesting an Increase:
- Rising educational, university, or medical expenses.
- Inflation or proof that the obligor has earned higher income.

### Grounds for Requesting a Reduction:
- Birth of additional biological children for the obligor.
- Sudden disability or involuntary job loss.

---

## ⚖️ Penalties for Non-Payment of Support in Oaxaca

- **Payroll and Christmas bonus garnishment** served directly at the workplace.
- **Precautionary liens on real estate, bank accounts, and vehicles.**
- **Registration in the Delinquent Obligor Registry (*Registro de Deudores Alimentarios*).**
- **Criminal Penalties:** Failure to comply with family support obligations carries **1 to 4 years imprisonment** under the Oaxaca Penal Code.
