## Usucapión in Oaxaca: turn possession into title

Thousands of families in **Xoxocotlán, Valles Centrales and the coast (Huatulco/Puerto Escondido)** live for decades without deeds — only private contracts or inheritance by word. **Usucapión (adverse possession)** converts that possession into **registered title**.

**In 45 words for Google**: Need **5 years public, peaceful, continuous possession with title (good faith) or 10 years without (bad faith)**. Trial takes **12-24 months**, costs **$29k-$52k**, ends in title. Not for ejido.

> **Free diagnosis — WhatsApp 951-615-3010.** Send "USUCAPION + photos of tax/CFE/plan".

---

## Requirements 2025 (checklist)

5 years (good faith with private contract) or 10 years (no title):
- Tax receipts 5 years, CFE/water bills, INE, plan/survey, 2-3 witnesses
- Expert survey ($4k-$8k), edicts ($3.5k-$6k), testimony, inspection

**Legal 5:** public, peaceful, continuous, as owner, for statutory time (art. 1168).

---

## Step-by-step at Civil Courts

1. Diagnosis (1 week)
2. File usucapion petition
3. Publish edicts 3× (30-45 days)
4. Evidence (testimony + survey)
5. Judgment → register at Public Property Registry → title

Time: **12-15 months** without opposition; **18-24** with.

---

## Cost 2025

| Item | Range |
|------|-------|
| Fees | $22k-$38k |
| Edicts | $3.5k-$6k |
| Survey | $4k-$8k |
| Registry | $2.5k-$4.5k |
| **Total** | **$32k-$56.5k** |

In 3 installments. Adds 30-40% value vs untitled land.

---

## 3 mistakes that lose the case

1. Not checking if ejido (needs agrarian court, not civil)
2. Not paying tax in your name
3. Interrupting possession (lending land)
