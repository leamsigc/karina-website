Purchasing real estate in **Oaxaca City, Puerto Escondido, Mazunte, or Huatulco** is an exciting investment. However, Mexican real estate law involves distinct property regimes: **Private Property (*Propiedad Privada*), Ejidal Land, and Communal Land (*Propiedad Comunal*)**.

Hiring an independent **Real Estate Lawyer and Notary Closing Service** is the most crucial step to ensure clear title ownership, avoid fraud, and protect your capital.

---

## 🔍 Legal Due Diligence: Protecting Your Investment

Before executing purchase contracts or wiring earnest deposits, our firm performs a rigorous legal title audit:

1. **IFREO Public Registry Search:** Verification at the Public Registry of State Property (*IFREO*) to confirm authentic seller ownership, chain of title, and absence of liens or mortgages.
2. **Agrarian Land Status Audit:** Verifying that the parcel is NOT encumbered as ejidal or communal land without official National Agrarian Registry (*RAN*) privatization decrees.
3. **Municipal Zoning & Tax Verification:** Confirming municipal permits, property tax clearance, and water fee compliance.

---

## 📜 Public Notary Closings & Title Deeds (*Escrituración*)

In Mexico, Public Notaries (*Notarios Públicos*) are government-appointed legal officers required for final title deeds:

- **Bilingual Purchase Agreements:** Drafted with protective penalty clauses and seller representations.
- **Lien-Free Certificates (*Certificado de Libertad de Gravamen*):** Required before notary signing.
- **Closing Taxes & Fees:** Calculating buyer Acquisition Tax (*ISAI*) and seller Capital Gains Tax (*ISR*).

---

## 🏖️ Coastal Restricted Zone Bank Fideicomisos for Foreigners

Under Article 27 of the Mexican Constitution, non-Mexican citizens acquiring real estate within **50 km of the coastline (Puerto Escondido, Huatulco, Zipolite)** must use a **Bank Trust (*Fideicomiso*)**:

- We secure the mandatory permit from the Ministry of Foreign Affairs (*SRE*).
- We structure the 50-year renewable Bank Fideicomiso granting the foreign buyer full ownership rights, including the right to sell, lease, or pass the property to designated heirs.
