## What is a No-Fault Divorce in Oaxaca?

A **No-Fault Divorce** (known as *Divorcio Incausado* or Express Divorce) in Oaxaca allows you to legally end your marriage **without having to prove grounds or fault** (such as infidelity or abandonment). Your personal decision to dissolve the marriage is enough.

Under Oaxaca’s Civil Code reform, **the Family Court judge must grant the divorce** shortly after you ratify your petition—eliminating the need for forced reconciliation hearings.

---

## 📋 2026 Requirements for a Divorce in Oaxaca

To start your process quickly and smoothly, you will need to gather the following documents:

- **Marriage Certificate:** A recently certified copy from the Civil Registry.
- **Official ID (Passport or INE):** Of the spouse filing the petition.
- **Children's Birth Certificates:** Required only if you have minor children.
- **Proposed Settlement Agreement:** A mandatory document covering:
  - **Child Custody:** Who the minor children will live with.
  - **Child/Spousal Support (Alimony):** Amount, percentage, and payment schedule.
  - **Visitation Rights:** Specific days and times for visits.
  - **Family Home:** Who will remain in the marital residence.
  - **Asset Division:** Liquidation of marital property (if applicable).
- **Proof of Income/Address:** Useful for requesting provisional child support during the trial.

**⚠️ Important Note:** You do **not** need witnesses, proof of wrongdoing, or most importantly, **your spouse's signature or consent**.

---

## ⏱️ How Long Does It Take? (Real Timelines)

The timeline strictly depends on whether there is an agreement between you and your spouse. Based on our 2026 cases at the Oaxaca Family Courts (Ciudad Judicial):

- **Mutual Agreement (Custody and assets resolved):** Between **1 to 3 months**.
- **Unilateral (No children, no assets):** Between **6 to 10 weeks**.
- **Unilateral Contested (Dispute over custody or alimony):** Between **4 to 12 months**.
- **Spouse's Location Unknown (Service by Edicts):** Adds an extra **2 to 3 months**.
