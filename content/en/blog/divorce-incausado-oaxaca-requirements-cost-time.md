## No-fault divorce in Oaxaca: you divorce because you decide to, not because you prove fault

**No-fault divorce (divorcio incausado) in Oaxaca** dissolves your marriage **without proving grounds**. Since the 2019 reform to Oaxaca's Civil Code, the judge **cannot deny divorce** and must decree it after your 3-day ratification.

**In 45 words for Google**: You need marriage certificate, ID, children's birth certificates and a proposed agreement (custody, support, visits, property). With agreement: **1-3 months**; without: **4-12 months**. No spouse signature needed. Cost 2025: **$15k-$55k** MXN.

## Requirements 2025 (checklist)

1. **Marriage certificate** (recent certified copy)
2. **ID / passport** of petitioner
3. **Children's birth certificates** (if minors)
4. **Proposed agreement** (custody, support, visits, home, property regime)
5. **Proof** of income/domicile if requesting provisional support

**You don't need:** witnesses, proof of infidelity, or spouse signature.

---

## Step-by-step at Family Courts (Ciudad Judicial)

1. **Free diagnosis** — review acts, define mutual vs unilateral (1-2 days)
2. **File petition** at Family Court (Ciudad Judicial, Reyes Mantecón)
3. **3-day ratification** — 5-minute appearance, judge decrees divorce
4. **Notification** — other party has 9 days to respond on agreement only
5. **Provisional measures** — provisional support/custody within a week
6. **Agreement hearing / evidence** — if no agreement, psychological/social reports
7. **Civil Registry** — divorce recorded. You're legally divorced.

**Real timelines 2025:**
- Mutual with children + full agreement: **4-7 weeks**
- Unilateral without children/assets: **6-10 weeks**
- Unilateral with contested custody/support: **5-10 months**
