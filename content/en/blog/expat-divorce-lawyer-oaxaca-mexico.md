For expats, dual citizens, and international couples residing in the United States, Canada, or Europe who married in Oaxaca (or whose spouse resides in Mexico), **obtaining a divorce does not require traveling to Mexico**.

Through **Mexican Consular Power of Attorney (*Poder Notarial Consular*)**, our bilingual legal team handles your entire divorce proceeding remotely before the Family Courts of Oaxaca.

---

## ✈️ How to File a Remote Expat Divorce in Oaxaca

Our firm streamlines cross-border divorce litigation into 4 simple steps:

1. **Virtual Case Evaluation:** We review marriage jurisdiction, child custody arrangements, and Mexican real estate assets.
2. **Drafting the Power of Attorney:** We prepare the exact legal text for a *Special Power of Attorney for Unilateral Divorce*.
3. **Consular Execution:** You attend an appointment at the nearest Mexican Consulate in your home country to execute and notarize the instrument.
4. **Filing & Decree in Oaxaca:** You send the original document to our office in Oaxaca via international courier, and we initiate court proceedings.

---

## ⚖️ Required Documents for Expat Divorce Filings

- **Marriage Certificate:** Certified copy from the Oaxaca Civil Registry, or foreign certificate **apostilled and officially translated into Spanish**.
- **Valid Government ID:** Passport or official photo ID.
- **Consular Power of Attorney:** Notarized by a Mexican Consular Officer.
- **Proposed Settlement Agreement:** Covering Mexican property division, spousal maintenance, and child custody if applicable.

---

## 🌐 Enforcing Your Mexican Divorce Decree Abroad

Upon final judgment by the Oaxaca Family Court Judge:
- We secure official marginal registration on your Civil Registry marriage record.
- We obtain certified court copies of the final divorce decree.
- We process the **State Apostille of Oaxaca** to ensure seamless recognition (*Exequatur*) by foreign courts and immigration agencies.
