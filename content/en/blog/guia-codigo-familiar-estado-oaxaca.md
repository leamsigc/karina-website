# Oaxaca Family Code: 2026 Practical Guide

The **Family Code of the State of Oaxaca** governs all domestic relations, marital assets, paternity, child custody, and support within the jurisdiction. Understanding these statutory provisions is vital when navigating family court proceedings in Oaxaca.

---

## 1. No-Fault Divorce (*Divorcio Incausado*)

Under Oaxaca statutory law, divorce no longer requires proving marital fault or obtaining mutual consent:

- **Unilateral Intent**: Either spouse can initiate divorce proceedings independently.
- **Regulatory Agreement**: The petitioner presents a settlement proposal covering child custody, visitation schedules, alimony, and marital asset liquidation.
- **Resolution Timeline**: Most uncontested proceedings finalize within **1 to 3 months**.

---

## 2. Child Support & Mandatory Payroll Garnishment

Child support in Oaxaca is strictly enforced under public interest standards:

1. **Proportionality Principle**: Awards reflect the financial ability of the obligor and the verified needs of the dependent.
2. **Immediate Withholding**: Courts issue immediate payroll garnishment orders (typically **15% to 35% per child**) served directly to employers.
3. **Retroactive Support**: Support claims can backdate to birth or legal paternity recognition.

---

## 3. Child Custody & Parental Rights

Judicial decisions strictly prioritize the **Best Interests of the Child**:

- **Patria Potestad**: Shared legal rights and parental duties retained by both parents.
- **Physical Custody**: Primary residence of the child, which may be sole or joint.
- **Visitation Rights**: Guaranteed schedules to preserve emotional bonds with the non-custodial parent.
