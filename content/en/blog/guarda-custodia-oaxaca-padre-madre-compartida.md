# Child Custody & Joint Custody in Oaxaca: Legal Guide & Visitation Trials (2026)

Under the **Civil Code of the State of Oaxaca**, child custody (*guarda y custodia*) determinations strictly prioritize the **Best Interest of the Child**, ensuring that minors grow up in a safe, stable, and loving environment.

---

## 📊 Core Distinction: Parental Authority (*Patria Potestad*) vs. Custody (*Guarda y Custodia*)

Confusing these two legal terms is common in Mexican family law. Here is the legal breakdown:

| Legal Feature | Parental Authority (*Patria Potestad*) | Physical Custody (*Guarda y Custodia*) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Legal Definition** | The overall bundle of rights and duties regarding legal representation and child asset administration. | Daily care, physical residence, supervision, and day-to-day cohabitation with the child. |
| **Ownership** | Belongs to **both parents equally** (unless terminated by a final court judgment). | Can be granted **solely to one parent** or **jointly shared between both**. |
| **Impact of Divorce** | **Is not lost** merely due to marital separation or divorce. | Determined via **settlement agreement or court trial** defining where the child lives. |

---

## ⚖️ Types of Custody in Oaxaca Family Courts

1. **Joint Custody (*Custodia Compartida*):** Preferred arrangement where parents alternate equal residential periods and jointly assume direct upbringing duties.
2. **Sole Custody (*Custodia Monoparental*):** The child resides primarily with one parent, establishing a formal **Visitation & Coexistence Schedule** for the non-custodial parent.
3. **Provisional Custody (*Custodia Provisional*):** Temporary protective measure issued in the early days of a lawsuit to safeguard the child while evidence is gathered.

---

## 👨‍👧 Can Fathers Obtain Sole or Joint Custody in Oaxaca?

**Yes.** Supreme Court of Justice (SCJN) precedents strictly prohibit automatic maternal preference based on gender stereotypes. Family judges evaluate objectively:

- **Verifiable emotional and financial stability.**
- **Adequate, safe home living environment.**
- **Actual time availability** to attend to educational and healthcare needs.
- **Respect for visitation rights** (parental alienation or obstruction can trigger forfeiture of custody).

---

## 📝 Mandatory Court Evidence in Custody Trials

To issue a final custody ruling, Oaxaca Family Court judges order:
1. **Psychological Evaluation:** Emotional assessment of both parents and the minor child.
2. **Socio-Economic & Social Work Study:** Physical home inspection by court social workers.
3. **Child Interview:** Conducted in a safe setting appropriate to the child's age and cognitive development.
