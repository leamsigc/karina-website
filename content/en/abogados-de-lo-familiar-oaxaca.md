## Frequently Asked Questions

### How long does a child support lawsuit take in Oaxaca?
While full trials can span several months, **provisional child support** is granted by the judge on an urgent basis within days of accepting the initial petition.

### Can wages or assets be garnished from a parent who fails to pay child support?
Yes. Oaxaca law authorizes direct wage garnishment orders issued to employers, as well as precautionary liens on real estate, vehicles, and bank accounts to guarantee support.
