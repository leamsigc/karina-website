## Our Core Family Law Services in Oaxaca

### 1. No-Fault Divorce (*Divorcio Incausado*)
In Oaxaca, either spouse can petition for divorce at any time without establishing grounds or obtaining permission from the other party. We handle:
- Drafting of the **Divorce Proposal (*Propuesta de Convenio*)** detailing child custody, visitation schedules, support payments, and property distribution.
- Representation in judicial hearings before the Oaxaca Family Courts (*Juzgados de lo Familiar*).
- Execution and official registration of the final divorce decree in the Civil Registry (*Registro Civil*).

### 2. Child Custody & Parental Rights (*Guarda y Custodia*)
Protecting the best interest of your children (*Interés Superior de la Niñez*) is paramount. We advocate for:
- Joint or sole physical and legal custody arrangements.
- Enforceable international visitation rights (*Régimen de Visitas y Convivencia*).
- Cross-border relocation authorization and prevention of unlawful child retention.

### 3. Child Support & Alimony (*Pensión Alimenticia*)
In Oaxaca, child support is calculated based on the needs of the child and the financial capability of the supporting parent (typically between 20% to 50% of verifiable income). We provide:
- Accurate income calculation for self-employed individuals, business owners, or foreign earners.
- Court petitions for modification, increase, or enforcement of child support orders.

### 4. Marital Property & Asset Division
If you purchased real estate, vehicles, or business assets during marriage under Community Property (*Sociedad Conjugal*) or Separate Property (*Separación de Bienes*), we ensure your financial rights are strictly enforced under Mexican civil law.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### Can I get divorced in Oaxaca if I got married outside of Mexico?
Yes. If either you or your spouse resides in Oaxaca, local Family Courts have jurisdiction. The foreign marriage certificate must be apostilled (or legalized) and translated into Spanish by an official certified court translator (*perito traductor*), which our office coordinates for you.

### Do I have to travel to Oaxaca for the court hearings?
Not necessarily. By granting a formal Power of Attorney (*Poder Notarial para Pleitos y Cobranzas*) through a Mexican Consulate or local notary, Attorney Karina Orocio can represent your legal interests directly in court on your behalf.
